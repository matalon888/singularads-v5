## CAMERA & MOTION

- A `CatmullRomCurve3` THROUGH TWELVE WAYPOINTS, **~150 WORLD UNITS APART**. PACKED ANY
  TIGHTER, SETS BLEED INTO EACH OTHER AND THE CAMERA FLIES THROUGH SCENERY INSTEAD OF
  APPROACHING IT — **ONE SET PER VIEW IS THE WHOLE POINT.**
- EACH SET IS ANCHORED ~62 UNITS *AHEAD* OF ITS WAYPOINT, SO IT EMERGES FROM THE FOG,
  GROWS, AND IS PASSED. THE PATH EXTRAPOLATES PAST ITS END SO THE FINAL CHAPTER IS
  APPROACHED RATHER THAN OCCUPIED.
- **PACING**: EACH SEGMENT IS SMOOTHSTEPPED, SO THE CAMERA ACCELERATES OUT OF A CHAPTER,
  CRUISES, AND SETTLES INTO THE NEXT. A CONSTANT GLIDE HAS NO CHARACTER.
- **FOG** `fogExp2` WHITE AT `0.006` — NEIGHBOURING CHAPTERS FADE OUT INSTEAD OF BEING
  CUT OFF. THIS IS WHAT MAKES IT A WORLD RATHER THAN OBJECTS ON A BACKGROUND.
- **SMOOTH SCROLL** VIA LENIS, SO TRAVEL HAS INERTIA AND WEIGHT.
- **THE VERB**: A LIGHT FOLLOWS THE CURSOR THROUGH THE WORLD AND THE SOLIDS RESPOND TO
  IT. AT THE SINGULAR POINT, CLICKING SCATTERS THE CORE.
- **HUD**: A CHAPTER RAIL DOWN THE LEFT EDGE NAMING WHERE YOU ARE — THE THING THAT
  TURNS SCROLLING INTO TRAVELLING — PLUS A PROGRESS THREAD AND A "SCROLL TO TRAVEL" CUE
  SHOWN ONCE.
