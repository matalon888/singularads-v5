BUILD A FULL, MULTI-SECTION, FULLY RESPONSIVE MARKETING SITE FOR **SINGULAR**, AN
AI-POWERED PROGRAMMATIC AD EXCHANGE. THE NAMED DESIGN LANGUAGE IS **"SOLID LIGHT"** —

EXTRUDED, SOFTLY-LIT SOLID OBJECT** YOU COULD PICK UP AND TURN OVER. NOTHING IS FLAT.
NOTHING IS A LINE DRAWING. THE MOOD IS EXPENSIVE, TACTILE, WEIGHTLESS AND ALIVE: INK

ACCENT SOLIDS, AND CONTINUOUS MOTION IN EVERY SINGLE VIEWPORT.

THE THESIS OF THE BRAND IS **CONVERGENCE** — MILLIONS OF SCATTERED SIGNALS COLLAPSING
INTO ONE SINGULAR POINT. EVERY 3D OBJECT ON THE PAGE IS A VARIATION ON THAT ONE IDEA:
MANY BLOCKS → ONE CORE.

## 3D SYSTEM — THIS IS THE POINT OF THE SITE

THREE INDEPENDENT LAYERS OF DIMENSIONALITY, ALL PRESENT ON EVERY PAGE VIEW:

1. **REAL WEBGL (REACT-THREE-FIBER + DREI).** THE HERO IS A FULL-VIEWPORT WHITE STUDIO
   CONTAINING ~150 ROUNDED-BOX "IMPRESSION BLOCKS" IN THE FOUR ACCENT MATERIALS PLUS
   WHITE, SUSPENDED IN A SPHERICAL FIELD, DRIFTING AND SLOWLY SPINNING. AS THE PAGE
   SCROLLS THEY **CONVERGE**: EACH BLOCK LERPS FROM ITS SCATTERED POSITION TOWARD THE
   ORIGIN WHILE THE CAMERA DOLLIES IN, UNTIL THE FIELD RESOLVES INTO A SINGLE DENSE
   CORE. LIGHTING IS A SOFT KEY + TWO TINTED RIMS; THE GROUND IS AN INVISIBLE
   SHADOW-CATCHING PLANE. MOUSE MOVEMENT PARALLAXES THE CAMERA. THE CANVAS IS `dpr`
   CAPPED AT 2 AND PAUSES WHEN OFF-SCREEN.
2. **CSS 3D (`transform-style: preserve-3d`).** EVERY CARD, TILE, BADGE AND PANEL LIVES
   IN A PERSPECTIVE CONTEXT AND TILTS TOWARD THE POINTER (`±10°`) WITH A SPRING, LIFTING
   `-6PX` AND DEEPENING ITS SHADOW. NOTHING ON THIS SITE IS A FLAT RECTANGLE.
3. **DIMENSIONAL SVG ILLUSTRATIONS.** THE ONLY ILLUSTRATION STYLE IS **ISOMETRIC
   EXTRUDED SOLIDS**: EVERY SHAPE IS DRAWN AS A TOP FACE (LIGHT TINT), A LEFT FACE
   (MID TINT) AND A RIGHT FACE (DARK TINT) ON A TRUE 2:1 ISOMETRIC GRID, WITH A SOFT
   ELLIPTICAL CONTACT SHADOW BENEATH. STROKES ARE `0` — FORM COMES FROM FACE SHADING
   ALONE. THERE MUST BE **AT LEAST 24 DISTINCT ILLUSTRATIONS**, ONE PER CONCEPT, EACH
   INDEPENDENTLY ANIMATED (FLOATING SLABS, ORBITING NODES, DRAWING PATHS, PULSING
   CORES, COUNTING BARS). RECURRING MOTIFS: **STACKED SLABS AS INVENTORY**, **ORBITING
   NODES**, **CONVERGING RAYS**, **A SINGLE GRADIENT-FILLED CUBE** AS THE MOMENT OF
   CONVERGENCE.
